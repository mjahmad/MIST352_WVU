WHAT IS THIS APP?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Farm & Garden Planner is a full-featured console application that
combines real-time NOAA weather data with a smart garden management
system. It helps farmers and gardeners:

  • See today's and this week's weather forecast
  • Know exactly what to plant based on YOUR location and climate
  • Visualize your garden layout in ASCII art
  • Decide whether to water, plant, or protect your crops today
  • Calculate frost dates from real historical NOAA data
  • Plan with the moon phases (biodynamic gardening)
  • Compare this year's weather to historical averages
  • Track your garden journal and harvest countdowns

The app uses TWO live NOAA data sources:
  1. api.weather.gov  — Real 7-day US weather forecast (no API key needed)
  2. NOAA GHCN       — Historical weather records from thousands of stations

HOW TO RUN THE APP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Requirements:
  • .NET 10.0 SDK installed (dotnet.microsoft.com)
  • Internet connection (for live NOAA data)

Steps:
  1. Open a terminal in the Project_2 folder
  2. Run:  dotnet run
  3. Enter your location when prompted (e.g., "Morgantown, WV")
     or press ENTER for the default (Morgantown, WV)
  4. Wait ~15–30 seconds for data to load from NOAA

Note: If NOAA APIs are slow or unavailable, the app falls back to
      demo weather data automatically so it always works offline.

MENU OPTIONS — FULL FEATURE LIST
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[1]  WEATHER DASHBOARD & 7-DAY FORECAST
     ─────────────────────────────────────
     Displays a full color-coded weather dashboard including:
     • Today's detailed weather card (temp, wind, rain, humidity)
     • ASCII bar chart comparing 7-day highs and lows
     • Full 7-day grid with all metrics
     • Garden-at-a-Glance summary: frost risk, heat waves,
       ideal conditions for specific plant types

     DATA PULLED: temperature (high/low), wind speed/direction,
     precipitation probability, relative humidity, weather description,
     detailed forecast text.

[2]  PLANT RECOMMENDATIONS FOR YOUR AREA
     ─────────────────────────────────────
     Uses NOAA historical data to determine your USDA Hardiness Zone,
     then recommends plants suited to your climate and this month.

     Shows:
     • Your USDA Zone (derived from historical minimum temperatures)
     • Plants you can start planting RIGHT NOW this month
     • Full 12-month planting calendar for all suitable plants
       (P = Plant month, H = Harvest month)
     • Water needs, sun needs, days to harvest for each plant

     PLANT DATABASE: 19 plants — vegetables, herbs, and flowers.

[3]  VIEW & MANAGE MY GARDEN
     ─────────────────────────────────────
     First shows a SCENIC VIEW with ASCII art for each plant type
     in your garden, then opens the interactive grid manager.

     Grid Manager features:
     • 6×10 plot grid (60 total plots)
     • Add plants by row/col with optional notes
     • Remove plants
     • View plant details + real-time growth progress bar
     • Watering and care advice based on today's forecast
     • Garden is SAVED automatically to garden_state.json

[4]  GARDEN CARE ADVISOR (WATER / PLANT?)
     ─────────────────────────────────────
     Answers the daily questions every farmer has:
     • Should I water today? (checks rain chance, humidity)
     • Should I plant today? (checks temp, wind, conditions)
     • What plants are ideal for today's exact temperatures?
     • Pest & disease risk based on humidity and heat
     • Day-by-day task list for the whole week:
       [Water] [FROST COVER] [Mulch/Shade] [Stake plants]

[5]  FROST DATE CALCULATOR
     ─────────────────────────────────────
     Uses actual NOAA GHCN historical data to calculate:
     • Last spring frost date (from last year's data)
     • First fall frost date
     • Growing season length in days
     • Days left in the growing season (if currently active)
     • Monthly frost risk table (None / Low / Moderate / High)
     • Frost protection tips
     • ALERT if any day in the 7-day forecast has frost risk

[6]  COMPANION PLANTING GUIDE
     ─────────────────────────────────────
     • Analyzes YOUR garden for companion plant conflicts/synergies
     • Suggests missing companion plants for each crop you have
     • Top 10 proven companion pairings with benefits explained
     • Three Sisters garden guide (Corn + Beans + Squash)
     • Browse any plant to see its full companion list

[7]  MOON PHASE PLANTING GUIDE  [WOW FEATURE]
     ─────────────────────────────────────
     Biodynamic farmers have planted by moon phases for centuries.
     This feature uses precise astronomical calculations to show:
     • Tonight's exact moon phase (Day X of 29.5-day cycle)
     • ASCII moon visualization + progress bar
     • Specific planting advice for the current phase
     • Best plant types to start RIGHT NOW
     • 14-day lunar calendar with planting recommendations
     • Full explanation of all 8 moon phases and their meaning

     The phase is calculated using the Julian Day method referenced
     from the known New Moon of January 6, 2000.

[8]  WEATHER vs. HISTORICAL COMPARISON  [WOW FEATURE]
     ─────────────────────────────────────
     Compares this week's live forecast against NOAA historical
     averages from last year:
     • Avg high/low this week vs. historical average for this month
     • "ABOVE/BELOW normal" indicators with color coding
     • Day-by-day temperature deviation bar chart
     • Full 12-month historical table (avg high, low, precip, snow,
       frost days)
     • Climate insights: "Running 6°F hotter than normal — consider
       shade cloth and extra watering"

[9]  GARDEN JOURNAL & HARVEST TRACKER  [WOW FEATURE]
     ─────────────────────────────────────
     Part farm diary, part harvest countdown dashboard.

     Harvest Tracker:
     • Shows every plant in your garden with a progress bar
     • Color-coded: green = ready, yellow = growing, cyan = early
     • Alerts when plants are READY TO HARVEST
     • Shows days grown vs. days to harvest for each plot

     Journal:
     • Log entries by type: observation / harvest / issue / note
     • Entries tagged with date, plant name, and type
     • Color-coded log view (green=harvest, red=issue, cyan=observation)
     • Summary stats: total entries, harvest count, issue count
     • Entries saved permanently to garden_journal.txt

[10] BROWSE ALL PLANT PROFILES (ASCII ART)
     ─────────────────────────────────────
     Lists all 19 plants by category with key stats, then lets you
     pull up a full profile for any plant showing:
     • Custom ASCII art visualization
     • All growing details (zone, temp range, water, sun, timing)
     • Companion plants and plants to avoid
     • Expert planting tip

HOW TO PRESENT THIS TO AN AUDIENCE

RECOMMENDED DEMO FLOW (7–10 minutes):

  STEP 1 — Open the app, enter your city (or audience's city).
             While it loads, explain what NOAA is and why real data
             matters vs. made-up placeholder numbers.

  STEP 2 — Show the main menu. Point out color coding and [WOW] tags.
             Build the audience's curiosity before diving in.

  STEP 3 — Go to [1] Weather Dashboard.
             → "This is pulling LIVE data from api.weather.gov
               right now. The bar chart is color-coded by temperature."
             → Point out frost risk line if present.

  STEP 4 — Go to [2] Plant Recommendations.
             → "The app figured out we're in Zone 6 — from actual
               NOAA temperature records, not a lookup table."
             → Show the planting calendar. Explain P/H columns.

  STEP 5 — Go to [3] Garden View.
             → Add a tomato at Row 2, Col 3.
             → Add a marigold at Row 2, Col 4.
             → Show the ASCII scenic view.
             → "This garden saves between sessions — open it tomorrow,
               your data is still here."

  STEP 6 — Go to [7] Moon Phase Guide. [WOW]
             → "Biodynamic farmers have used lunar planting for
               1,000 years. We calculate the exact phase using
               the Julian Day method — real astronomy."

  STEP 7 — Go to [8] Weather vs. History. [WOW]
             → "Is this April hotter than usual? We compare against
               last year's NOAA station data to find out — and tell
               you what it means for your crops."

  STEP 8 — Go to [9] Journal & Harvest Tracker. [WOW]
             → "Watch the progress bar fill as days pass. When it
               hits 100% — it's harvest time. The journal logs
               everything: harvests, issues, observations."

  STEP 9 — Go to [5] Frost Date Calculator.
             → "Last spring frost, first fall frost, your growing
               season length — all from real NOAA station data
               downloaded live."

  CLOSING — "10 features, real government weather data, no GUI
             framework, no paid APIs, zero API keys. Just .NET 10
             and code."
